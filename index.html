Computer Science 1815 – 1852

Ada Lovelace

First computer programmer in history

She wrote the world's first computer algorithm for Charles Babbage's mechanical computer, the Analytical Engine. Crucially, she was the first to realize that computers could do more than just compute numbers—foreseeing that they could create music, art, and complex graphics if programmed correctly.
